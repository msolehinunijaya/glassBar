# GlassBar promo: shot list

60 s, 16:9, 128 BPM synthesized house bed (1 bar = 1.875 s, 32 bars). All UI is real GlassBar, captured from the running app with demo data.

**Look**
- **Wallpaper:** a slow-moving liquid aurora (violet, pink, cyan, warm orange) drawn in code.
- **Dock:** sits at the bottom centre. The wallpaper is blurred behind the glass, like the real acrylic.
- **Type:** Poppins (the app's own face).
- **Accent:** #0a84ff.

**Notes**
- The song on screen is a fictional demo track, "Glass Hours" by Nova Lane, with original lyric lines.
- Todos, notes, notifications and clipboard items are demo text. Your location is shown as Kuala Lumpur.
- I couldn't capture the pop-up banner window, because GlassBar suspends it when hidden. Shot 7 uses the real notification card from the Notifications panel instead.
- I only animate the drag itself (lifting and sliding real captured widgets). Recording the real drag was too slow to look smooth (~7 frames per second).
